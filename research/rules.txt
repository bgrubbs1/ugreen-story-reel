Project Requirements
What to Create:
Create an AI Skill for UGREEN AI NAS using the official UGOS Skill Creation Tool and the development resources provided in the Hackathon Starter Kit. Please download:
ai.ugreen.com/pages/hackathon-starter-kit
The Starter Kit provides instructions for setting up your development environment, creating Skills, and understanding supported NAS capabilities. You can also download the UGOS Skill Creation Tool directly from the Starter Kit.
All submitted Skills must comply with the supported capabilities and technical requirements specified in the official documentation. Skills must follow the official Uliya Skill Development Standard and use supported UGOS CLI capabilities through registered expert agents. Custom executable scripts, general-purpose programming runtimes, and unauthorized direct access to NAS resources are not supported.
Entrants are encouraged to use the AI NAS local AI model, but its use is not required. Properly authorized third-party models may also be used, subject to applicable rights, service terms, and the data restrictions outlined under Third Party Integrations.
Entrants are encouraged to join the UGREEN Community for technical guidance and support throughout the Hackathon.
Automate Your Workflow
- Turn repetitive work into executable AI workflows. Build an AI Skill that uses authorized NAS files and data, together with the file, document, knowledge, and system capabilities provided for the Hackathon, to organize content, extract information, generate summaries, and streamline repetitive tasks.
Bring Memories to Life
- Make family content and digital memories easier to find and enjoy. Build an AI Skill that organizes, searches, extracts text from, summarizes, or creates albums from authorized photos, videos, and documents stored on UGREEN AI NAS.
Unleash Your Creativity
- Turn your media library into a starting point for creativity.Build an AI Skill that helps photographers, editors, designers, and other content creators search, tag, select, organize, and review media stored on UGREEN AI NAS, making creative preparation faster and more efficient.
Open Innovation
- Combine the NAS, AI, media, file and knowledge capabilities provided for the challenge to build an original AI Skill that solves a real user need and runs on UGREEN AI NAS.

6. Judges & Criteria
Sponsor and Administrator reserve the sole right to determine the eligibility and judging methodologies for all submissions. This process may utilize expert panels, peer review, automated AI-driven analysis, or any combination thereof to ensure efficient, fair, and objective evaluation. Eligible submissions will be evaluated by a panel of judges selected by the Sponsor (the “Judges”). Final eligibility and judging decisions are made by humans. Judges may be employees of the sponsor or third parties, may or may not be listed individually on the Hackathon Website, and may change before or during either Judging Period. Judging may take place in one or more rounds with one or more panels of Judges, at the discretion of the sponsor.
Round 1 Judging:
The first stage will determine via pass/fail whether the ideas meet a baseline level of viability, in that the Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably proposes an AI NAS solution. Eligible Projects will then be scored according to the Judging Criteria below. Because Entrants do not have NAS access during the Round 1 Submission Period, Judges will evaluate each criterion based on the Round 1 Submission materials, including the PDF and ZIP Pre-test Skill Package, and the Project’s proposed functionality, planned use of UGREEN AI NAS capabilities, and validation plan. A lack of NAS runtime evidence will not, by itself, make a Submission ineligible or lower its Round 1 score. Up to thirty (30) of the highest-scoring eligible Projects will become Finalists.
Round 2 Judging:
All eligible Projects submitted during the Round 2 Submission Period will be evaluated based on their working Skill in the assigned UGREEN AI NAS environment, the video, and the documentation, including whether the ideas in the Round 1 Submission were delivered. Round 1 scores do not carry over. Winners are decided by Round 2 scores only.
Judging Criteria (Rounds 1 and 2): Entries will be judged on the following equally weighted criteria (the “Judging Criteria”), according to the sole and absolute discretion of the Judges:
User Value:
Whether the Skill addresses a clear user problem and gives users a clear workflow, useful feedback, and a meaningful result.
Innovation & Differentiation:
Whether the Skill offers a substantive new function, interaction, or workflow that differs from conventional AI tools and existing solutions.
Technical Quality:
Whether the Skill demonstrates a sound orchestration approach, appropriate use of supported CLI capabilities and expert agents, proper handling of permissions and errors, and reliable execution appropriate to its development stage.
AI NAS Fit & Validation:
Whether the Skill makes meaningful use of UGREEN AI NAS capabilities, including relevant local data, storage, computing, privacy, or other supported capabilities, rather than merely running on NAS.
If a Finalist is unable or ineligible to proceed, the Sponsor may invite the next highest-scoring eligible Project in its place.
The scores from the Judges will determine the potential winners of the applicable prizes. The Entrant(s) that are eligible for a Prize, and whose Submissions earn the highest overall scores based on the applicable Judging Criteria, will become potential winners of that Prize.
Tie Breaking
For each Prize listed below, if two or more Submissions are tied, the tied Submission with the highest score in the first applicable criterion listed above will be considered the higher scoring Submission. In the event any ties remain, this process will be repeated, as needed, by comparing the tied Submissions’ scores on the next applicable criterion. If two or more Submissions are tied on all applicable criteria, the panel of Judges will vote on the tied Submissions.
For the Most Creative Prize, if two or more Submissions are tied on the Innovation & Differentiation Judging Criterion, the tied Submission with the highest overall score will be considered the higher-scoring Submission. If any ties remain, the panel of Judges will vote on the tied Submissions.
